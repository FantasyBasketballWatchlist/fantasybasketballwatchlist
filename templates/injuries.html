How players come back

2009-10 to 2025-26

Every time a regular (20+ minutes per game) missed 5 or more team games in a row and came back the same season: his fantasy points per game for the rest of that season, as a share of his average before the absence.

Statuses come from the NBA's official injury report (every 15 minutes on game days); each player is shown on the latest report that included his team's game. Plays = how often players with that status played that day (2021-22 to 2025-26 reports). For players listed Out with an injury, the outlook is how many games in a row regulars listed Out with that kind of injury went on to miss (surgeries, tears and fractures: a median of 15). It's a typical case, not a medical timeline for this player: team updates beat it. The comeback numbers are background only: when we tried shrinking BPV for players just back from an absence, it didn't predict better, because BPV's recent-form rule catches the drop within about five games (method, Section 16).
