Ranked by team score

Team score = BPV of the team's best starting lineup, with empty starting spots (and starters worse than a free agent) counted as a free agent (a replacement-level player, N/A BPV). BPV is each player's adjusted value: expected fantasy points per game after accounting for missed games, so a 10-point edge in team score is about 10 points a day. Over repl. = BPV above what a free agent gives.

Teams are saved in this browser, so a refresh won't lose them. A player can be on only one team; adding him to another team moves him. Positions are ESPN's eligibility. This is BPV's opinion only; in backtests, BPV on its own didn't beat ESPN's rankings.
