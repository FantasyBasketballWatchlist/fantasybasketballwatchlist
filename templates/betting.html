Projection = his stat mix over his last 82 games, scaled to BPV's per-game value and adjusted for recent minutes, the opponent, back-to-backs and home/away. The range is the middle 80% of his likely outcomes. Its accuracy is tracked every night in "How accurate has BPV been?" on the Rankings page.

+1.0%

Best price at Virginia-type licensed books, market blend, injury-news and blowout filters: 773 bets, but the 95% range is -6% to +8%, so it can't be told apart from break-even.

Prices beating the other books' consensus an hour before tip-off: -0.9% to +1.3% depending on the threshold. Fast-moving value needs live prices, which this test didn't have.
