Read this first. Chances assume he plays (a bet on a player who sits is void). In testing on a season the model never saw, its projections beat each player's season average and its ranges were honest, but its over/under chances ran a little overconfident for points, rebounds and assists (and underconfident for fantasy points), and nothing has been tested against real sportsbook lines yet. Treat it as information, not a betting edge. How it was tested. Sports betting is 21+ and legal only in some states.

Projection = his stat mix over his last 82 games, scaled to BPV's per-game value and adjusted for recent minutes, the opponent, back-to-backs and home/away. The range is the middle 80% of his likely outcomes. Its accuracy is tracked every night in "How accurate has BPV been?" on the Rankings page.
