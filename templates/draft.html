Now = BPV of each team's best starting lineup so far, with empty starting spots filled by a free agent (a replacement-level player). Projected = the same after the rest of the draft, assuming every team takes the best BPV left, so draft position is accounted for. BPV is roughly expected fantasy points per game from that spot, so a 10-point edge is about 10 points a day. This is BPV's opinion only; in backtests, BPV on its own didn't beat ESPN's rankings.

The dashed line marks how many picks happen before your next turn: players above it will probably be gone if the other teams draft by BPV. Real drafters don't, so treat it as a rough guide. The draft is saved in this browser, so a refresh won't lose it.

Positions are ESPN's eligibility. The suggested pick is the best BPV left unless you're about to run out of picks for a starting spot, or a player within 1 BPV fills a scarce position you need. In backtests, adjusting player value by position didn't improve rankings, so position mainly matters for filling a legal lineup. These tips are rules of thumb and haven't been backtested.
