The dashed line marks how many picks happen before your next turn: players above it will probably be gone if the other teams draft by BPV. Real drafters don't, so treat it as a rough guide. The draft is saved in this browser under the league picked at the top, so a refresh won't lose it; start another league there (or on Your leagues) to run a second draft without losing this one.

Positions are ESPN's eligibility; a player listed at two positions can start at either. The player list is sorted by BPV alone, so taking players straight down it tends to pile up guards. The suggested pick accounts for position: it plays out the rest of the draft for each candidate and picks the one whose final roster gets the most out of daily lineups (PG, SG, SF, PF, C, G, F, 3 UTIL by default). Only players with a game that night can start, so on busy nights a roster with eight guards and one center leaves spots empty. In simulated drafts, following the suggestions gave rosters with every position covered and about 15–20 BPV more than following the list in the worst draft spots. Simulations aren't real drafts, so treat it as a guide.
