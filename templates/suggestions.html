Team strength = BPV of a team's best starting lineup (ESPN slots; dual-position players move to where they help most). Positions compare your best two players who can play each spot with the league's teams. Trade ideas are one-for-one swaps that raise your lineup; "likely yes" ones also give them a reason to accept: their lineup improves, they get as much BPV back, or ESPN's average draft position (how most owners value players) ranks the player they get higher. Health = your starters' expected share of remaining games (BPV availability), weighted by BPV. Minutes and shooting changes are the same ones as on the Waiver Wire page.
