# Season-long fantasy basketball projection/ranking sources: accuracy evidence and availability

Research date: 2026-09-29. About 15 tool calls. Reddit could not be searched or fetched: the search tool refused reddit.com ("domains not accessible to our user agent"), and general web searches returned no indexed r/fantasybball threads. **Every claim about what the community thinks is therefore missing from these notes.**

## Q1: Is there independent evidence of accuracy (backtests, third-party comparisons)?

### Takeaway
I found no independent, season-long accuracy comparison of Hashtag Basketball, Basketball Monster, ESPN, Yahoo, CBS, RotoWire or NumberFire. The only peer-reviewed study found evaluates *daily* NBA fantasy projections from a one-month sample. It finds that professional projections beat naive forecasts only modestly, and that paid providers did not significantly beat a free one. None of the sites I checked publishes a self-reported accuracy number for NBA.

### Cited Findings
- **Döpke, Köhler & Tegtmeier, "Are they worth it? An evaluation of predictions for NBA 'Fantasy Sports'", Journal of Economics and Finance, vol. 48 (2024; online 2023).** They evaluated 1,658 projections from four projection providers, collected in February 2022. Professional forecasts reduce naive-forecast errors "only to a moderate extent" (a search summary put this at under 10%). The predictions are inefficient and in some cases biased. The free Daily Fantasy Fuel was not significantly beaten by any paid forecaster. The authors are independent academics. The sample is daily/DFS-style, not preseason season-long. — [RePEc abstract](https://ideas.repec.org/a/spr/jecfin/v48y2024i1d10.1007_s12197-023-09646-7.html); [Springer](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s12197-023-09646-7) (paywalled/redirect; the full text would not load, and ResearchGate returned 403). **I could not confirm the names of the four providers.**
- FantasyPros keeps a directory of NBA experts by site, including Yahoo, CBS, Basketball Monster, RotoWire and Razzball. That directory page shows **no NBA accuracy leaderboard or methodology**, unlike FantasyPros' NFL and MLB accuracy contests. — [FantasyPros NBA experts](https://www.fantasypros.com/experts/nba/)
- FantasyPros publishes consensus NBA rankings, including an ESPN-points version and a CBS-points version, for 2026-27. These are a possible benchmark or aggregate to include in a leaderboard. — [FantasyPros ESPN points rankings](https://www.fantasypros.com/nba/rankings/overall-points-espn.php); [CBS points](https://www.fantasypros.com/nba/rankings/overall-points-cbs.php)
- Basketball Monster: its FAQ and membership page make no accuracy claims. — [BBM FAQ](https://basketballmonster.com/Help.aspx); [BBM membership](https://basketballmonster.com/membership.aspx)
- Hashtag Basketball: its projections page makes no accuracy claims. — [Hashtag projections](https://hashtagbasketball.com/fantasy-basketball-projections)
- RotoWire: its "rankings vs projections" article describes the method (minutes, role, pace, analyst review) but makes no accuracy claims. — [RotoWire article](https://www.rotowire.com/basketball/article/fantasy-basketball-rankings-vs-projections-96956)
- CBS: its projections page carries a disclaimer: "CBS Sports makes no representation or warranty as to the accuracy..." — [CBS projections](https://www.cbssports.com/fantasy/basketball/projections/)
- ESPN: the rest-of-season projection articles by André Snellings (for example, the Dec 2024 update) make no accuracy claims. — [ESPN ROS projections 2024-25](https://www.espn.com/fantasy/basketball/story/_/id/42898411/fantasy-basketball-rankings-projections-points-h2h)
- Mike Clay's FSGA "most accurate preseason rankings" award (2013) is for **NFL**, not NBA. It says nothing about ESPN basketball projections. — [ESPN Press Room](https://espnpressroom.com/bio/mike-clay/)
- The claim that "source accuracy doesn't persist season to season, so averaging beats historical weighting" came up in search, but it comes from an **NFL DFS** analysis (11 seasons). It does not come from basketball. — [FantasyFootballAnalytics, Sep 2026](https://fantasyfootballanalytics.net/2026/09/we-analyzed-11-seasons-of-dfs-projections-heres-what-we-found.html)

### Inferences
- Claims that one source is "most accurate" for season-long NBA are, as far as I could find, unmeasured opinion. A leaderboard the user builds would be new evidence rather than a duplicate of existing work.
- By analogy only (the NFL finding plus the NBA paper's result that paid ≈ free), a consensus or average of sources is a reasonable baseline to include. So is a naive "last season per-game × expected games" forecast.

### Gaps
- r/fantasybball backtests and community consensus (for example, "Basketball Monster vs Hashtag") are unverified because Reddit is blocked for this tool. The user should search Reddit directly.
- The provider names in the Döpke et al. paper are unknown; the full text was inaccessible.
- I found no NumberFire/FanDuel Research, Yahoo, Razzball, RotoBaller or SportsLine NBA accuracy claims. (A search result about Yahoo's "projections upgrade" was about football.)

## Q2: Does each source support points leagues (ESPN-style H2H points)?

### Takeaway
Hashtag, CBS, RotoWire, FantasyPros and ESPN all have points-league content. Basketball Monster calculates "from your league settings" but does not advertise points support on the pages I checked. Most sources publish raw stat lines, so the user can apply ESPN's scoring (PTS 1, 3PM 1, FGA -1, FGM 2, FTA -1, FTM 1, REB 1, AST 2, STL 4, BLK 4, TOV -2) to any source that includes FGA/FGM, FTA/FTM and TOV.

### Cited Findings
- **Hashtag Basketball:** has a dedicated "Fantasy Basketball Points League Projections" section with customizable multipliers for each stat. — [Hashtag](https://hashtagbasketball.com/fantasy-basketball-projections)
- **Basketball Monster:** "Everything on the site is calculated from your league settings." Its draft tool supports Yahoo, ESPN, Fantrax, CBS, Sleeper and Ottoneu. The membership page is labeled "H2H/Roto" and does not explicitly mention points leagues. — [BBM FAQ](https://basketballmonster.com/Help.aspx); [BBM membership](https://basketballmonster.com/membership.aspx)
- **CBS:** free season projections include component stats plus a total FPTS column (CBS scoring, not ESPN's). — [CBS projections](https://www.cbssports.com/fantasy/basketball/projections/)
- **RotoWire:** publishes annual points-league rankings articles (2024, 2025-26, 2026-27) and site-specific rankings (Yahoo, CBS, etc.) with a projections view. — [RotoWire points rankings 2026-27](https://www.rotowire.com/basketball/article/fantasy-basketball-points-league-rankings-2026-27-134657); [RotoWire rankings (CBS H2H projections)](https://www.rotowire.com/basketball/rankings.php?leagueType=h2h&site=CBS&stats=projections)
- **ESPN:** publishes points-league ranking articles, including a "way-too-early" 2026-27 points ranking and a 10-team H2H points mock draft. — [ESPN way-too-early points rankings](https://www.espn.com/fantasy/basketball/story/_/id/48484688/fantasy-basketball-way-too-early-points-league-rankings-next-season-2026-27); [ESPN points mock](https://www.espn.com/fantasy/basketball/story/_/id/50014025/fantasy-basketball-mock-draft-10-team-h2h-points-league-nba-2026-27). The Snellings ROS projection table lists GP, MPG, PTS, AST, REB, 3PM, STL, BLK, TOV, FG% and FT%. **It shows percentages, not attempts**, so exact ESPN points cannot be rebuilt from it without FGA and FTA. — [ESPN ROS projections](https://www.espn.com/fantasy/basketball/story/_/id/42898411/fantasy-basketball-rankings-projections-points-h2h)
- **FantasyPros:** has ESPN-points and CBS-points consensus rankings. — [FantasyPros](https://www.fantasypros.com/nba/rankings/overall-points-espn.php)
- **RotoBaller:** has a "2026 Fantasy Basketball Projections (NBA)" page. I did not examine the details. — [RotoBaller](https://www.rotoballer.com/fantasy-basketball-projections-nba)

### Inferences
- For ESPN points scoring, FGA and FTA matter, at -1 each. Sources that publish only FG% and FT% (like the ESPN article table) cannot be scored exactly. Prefer sources with made/attempted columns (CBS lists FG and FT components; Hashtag and BBM show makes/attempts inside their tools).
- ESPN's in-app player projections are shown against league scoring inside the game. That is plausible but **not verified here**.

### Gaps
- I did not verify whether Yahoo's in-game preseason projections include attempts, or whether they can be pulled out.
- I did not fetch NumberFire/FanDuel Research NBA season-long projections; their existence and format are unverified.

## Q3: Free vs paid, cost, and how to capture preseason projections each year

### Takeaway
The cheapest full access is Hashtag at $2.50/mo; free users see only the top 30. Basketball Monster is a single $69.95/season membership with Excel/CSV-style export. CBS season projections look free. RotoWire's season-long plan is about $7.99/mo billed annually. To build a leaderboard, the user must snapshot each source's preseason projections just before opening night every year, because most pages overwrite with rest-of-season values.

### Cited Findings
- **Hashtag Basketball:** "Free users see the top 30 players only"; "Unlock full projections and our premium Yahoo, ESPN & Fantrax league tools for $2.50/mo." The page offers rest-of-season projections with 7/14/30-day blend options. **No separate preseason archive was visible.** — [Hashtag projections](https://hashtagbasketball.com/fantasy-basketball-projections)
- **Basketball Monster:** the official membership page lists one tier, "Monster Membership (H2H/Roto)", at $69.95 USD. It includes Josh Lloyd's full-season projection set plus a bonus projection set (search snippet: Kyle McKeown's), daily/short-term projections, Draft Monster, and a Chrome extension for ESPN. — [BBM membership](https://basketballmonster.com/membership.aspx). A third-party review claims $9.99/$19.99/$29.99 monthly tiers, **which conflicts with the official page**. Treat the official page as authoritative. — [sportbotai review](https://www.sportbotai.com/blog/tools/basketball-monster-review)
- **Basketball Monster export:** a search snippet from BBM's own pages said the Player Rankings page (per-game stats plus z-scores) and the Projections page (per-game stats) can be exported. I could not open the exact page to confirm it. — [BBM tutorials](https://basketballmonster.com/Tutorials.aspx); [BBM forum topic](https://basketballmonster.com/messagetopic.aspx?topic=24603). The FAQ says "Josh Projections, Bonus Projections, last season" are available "before the season". — [BBM FAQ](https://basketballmonster.com/Help.aspx)
- **RotoWire:** according to a search summary of its pricing page, Season-Long costs $14.99/mo or $7.99/mo billed annually, Daily $49.99/$24.99, and All Access $99.99/$39.99. Figures are from a search snippet and should be verified. The draft software is for subscribers only. Rankings pages reportedly have an "Export Table Data" option, which may be tier-limited. — [RotoWire pricing](https://www.rotowire.com/subscribe/pricing/); [RotoWire draft software](https://www.rotowire.com/basketball/draft-software.php); [RotoWire projections](https://www.rotowire.com/basketball/projections.php)
- **CBS Sports:** season projections live at a URL path under `/free/projections_season`, which suggests they are free. Columns include FG, FT, 3PM, REB, AST, STL, TO, BLK, PTS and FPTS. No export button was visible. The CBS app says its matchup projections are "powered by SportsLine". — [CBS projections](https://www.cbssports.com/fantasy/basketball/projections/); [CBS app](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/cbs-sports-fantasy/id658308834)
- **ESPN:** projections are published free in articles, as a table in HTML with the top 200 (Snellings). Rankings articles are also free. I found no export. — [ESPN](https://www.espn.com/fantasy/basketball/story/_/id/42898411/fantasy-basketball-rankings-projections-points-h2h)
- **FantasyPros:** free consensus rankings for ESPN points and CBS points. — [FantasyPros](https://www.fantasypros.com/nba/rankings/overall-points-espn.php)
- **Other sources found but not examined:** FTN rest-of-season projections ([FTN](https://ftnfantasy.com/nba/rest-of-season-projections)), LineupExperts preseason projections with a `Preseason` filter ([LineupExperts](https://www.lineupexperts.com/basketball/projections?flt_proj_time_period=Preseason)), FanScout ([fanscout.pro](https://fanscout.pro/projections)).

### Inferences
- Capture protocol (a suggestion, not sourced): in the week before opening night, save the HTML or CSV of each source to `data/preseason_projections/<season>/<source>.csv` with a timestamp. Use the BBM export; Hashtag premium; CBS HTML tables; the ESPN article table; FantasyPros ESPN-points consensus; and the RotoWire export if subscribed. Use the Wayback Machine to recover past seasons where it is archived. Score each source with Spearman correlation and MAE on total ESPN points (and per-game points plus games played) against actual results from nba_api.
- Because Hashtag's page shows rest-of-season values, a preseason snapshot has to be taken before the season starts. Past seasons may only be recoverable through web archives.

### Gaps
- Unverified: exact export formats for Hashtag premium and RotoWire; whether Yahoo or ESPN in-game projections can be exported; Fantrax, Razzball, RotoBaller, SportsLine and NumberFire pricing and formats; whether any source keeps an archive of past preseason projections.
