# Practical limits on profitably betting NBA player props in the US (as of 2026-10-21)

Source-quality note: regulator and trade-press sources (Covers, Front Office Sports, Legal Sports Report, iGaming Business) are reasonably reliable. Many hits on limits, exchanges and DFS payouts came from affiliate or SEO pages, and are flagged as weak where used. The findings come from about 20 searches and fetches. The Legal Sports Report Massachusetts page returned a 403 and was not read.

## 1. How quickly and severely do US-licensed sportsbooks limit winning prop bettors?

### Takeaway
Limiting is real and aimed at "advantage players". Regulators have now made it partly transparent (Massachusetts notice rule, Feb 2026). Operators say only about 1% of customers are limited. The dollar caps on limited prop accounts and how fast limits arrive are documented only by anecdote: reports range from $5–$100 per prop, often within weeks. A 1–5% prop edge at DraftKings, FanDuel, BetMGM and similar books should be treated as a short-lived, small-stakes opportunity, not a scalable one.

### Cited Findings
- **Massachusetts data (Sept 11, 2024 MGC roundtable):**
  - BetMGM said it limits about 1% of Massachusetts patrons, whom it calls "advantage players". Limits cut the bettor's "stake factor" from 100% of a market's limit to 50%, 25% or lower. BetMGM said decisions are based on behavior, not winnings.
  - Fanatics said "nearly half" of its limited group were net losers while restricted, and that over 90% of winning customers were never limited.
  - FanDuel said 0.043% of its 2023 Massachusetts wagers were placed at a player's maximum allowed amount, and that it offers about 37,000+ markets on an average day.
  - DraftKings cited "courtsiding" as a reason to limit.
  - Caesars said it bans customers for reasons other than beating the book, and those reasons are often not disclosed.
  - Commissioner Skinner noted that even a casual bettor could be deemed an advantage player depending on activity.
  - Source: [Covers, Sept 2024](https://www.covers.com/industry/massachusetts-limiting-sports-betting-debate-sharps-roundtable-september-2024)
- **MGC data request:** The MGC found 0.64% of Massachusetts bettors were limited as of December 2024. Its analysis found consistent winners were far more likely to be restricted, while consistent losers were more likely to have limits raised or kept. This is a secondhand summary of MGC data via search snippets from the [Covers NY bill article](https://www.covers.com/industry/new-york-legislation-looks-to-prohibit-sportsbooks-from-limiting-bettors-oct-1-2025) and [WSN](https://www.wsn.com/betting/new-york-is-considering-a-bill-to-end-limits-on-sports-bettors/). I did not see the primary MGC document.
- **Massachusetts notice rule (adopted unanimously Feb 26, 2026; effective June 1, 2026, waivers possible):**
  - Sportsbooks must give "timely notice", which commissioners interpret as within 48 hours, every time a bettor is limited.
  - The notice must give a "specific explanation" and name which markets are limited.
  - It is a transparency rule, not a ban on limiting.
  - The article says federally regulated prediction markets promote "no limiting", and that some sharps have moved to them.
  - Source: [Covers, Feb 2026](https://www.covers.com/industry/massachusetts-limited-sports-bettors-notice-timely-explain-february-2026)
- **New York A9125 "Fair Play Act" (introduced late Sept 2025 by Assemblymember Alex Bores):**
  - Would bar sportsbooks from limiting bettors for winning, with exceptions for suspicious activity and problem gambling.
  - Would require electronic notice within 24 hours.
  - Reporting says operators have dropped limits for hot bettors to under $1.
  - It was pending as of Oct 2025. I found no evidence that it passed.
  - Source: [Covers, Oct 1, 2025](https://www.covers.com/industry/new-york-legislation-looks-to-prohibit-sportsbooks-from-limiting-bettors-oct-1-2025)
- **Size and speed of prop limits (anecdotal, weak sources):**
  - Pro-bettor reports say DraftKings prop limits can fall to $5–$10 per prop for consistent winners who beat closing lines or bet soft numbers.
  - Reddit-reported prop maximums on limited accounts range from $12 to $100.
  - One first-person account describes a BetMGM limit after about one week, applied "in blocks".
  - Unrestricted ceilings quoted by guides: FanDuel props $1,000–$2,500 and DraftKings NBA props $1,000–$5,000. These are unverified.
  - Sources: [agentbets DraftKings props guide](https://agentbets.ai/guides/draftkings-prop-bets/); [sportbotai Reddit summary](https://www.sportbotai.com/blog/draftkings-max-wager-limit-reached-reddit); [Bleacher Nation, Oct 2025](https://www.bleachernation.com/betting/2025/10/17/maximum-bet-size/)
- **League pressure on role-player props (late Oct 2025):** After the federal indictments of Terry Rozier and others, NBA Commissioner Adam Silver said the league had asked sportsbooks to scale back props for players with limited minutes, such as two-way players. The Rozier case involved unders on his props in a 2023 game. Source: [Scripps/AP syndication, Oct 2025](https://www.scrippsnews.com/sports/nbas-rozier-billups-caught-up-in-fbi-gambling-investigation-reports-say)
- **Integrity reviews (Nov 2025):**
  - The NBA was reviewing with sportsbook partners how to limit manipulation-prone bets.
  - The NFL asked books to stop some props.
  - MLB set a nationwide plan with books to restrict pitch-level props.
  - Source: [Front Office Sports, Nov 14, 2025](https://frontofficesports.com/article/kalshi-adds-nba-prop-markets-as-betting-crackdowns-surge/)
- **Offshore spillover:** At the MGC roundtable, David Hill said Costa Rican books reported business returning because US players were being limited. Source: [Covers, Sept 2024](https://www.covers.com/industry/massachusetts-limiting-sports-betting-debate-sharps-roundtable-september-2024)

### Inferences
- With limits possibly in the $5–$100 range after detection, a 1–5% edge on limited accounts yields cents to a few dollars of expected value per bet. Total extractable value per account is probably bounded by how long the account survives before detection, not by bankroll.
- The detection signal operators describe is behavior: beating the close, betting soft or stale numbers, niche markets. A model that bets role-player props, which are the softest lines, is the most likely to trigger fast limits. These are also the markets the NBA has asked books to shrink.
- Two friends with separate accounts in their own names is allowed. Pooling money or one person betting through another's account is generally a terms-of-service violation, and possibly a regulatory one, so each person should bet only their own account. I have not verified this against a source.

### Gaps
- No primary data on time-to-limit for prop bettors specifically, such as a median number of days or bets.
- I found no book-by-book published maximums for NBA props at Caesars, BetRivers, ESPN Bet (now theScore Bet), Fanatics or Hard Rock.
- Status of NY A9125 in the 2026 session is unknown.
- I did not find Illinois or New Jersey regulator reports on limiting. Illinois held hearings, but I did not fetch them.

## 2. Sharp / market-making books and exchanges usable as reference prices

### Takeaway
For NBA props there is no single authoritative sharp price:
- Pinnacle (offshore, not legal for US residents) posts props at low limits.
- Circa posts only selected props.
- High-volume US books may be as sharp on props as either.
- The realistic no-limit venues are CFTC-regulated exchanges (Kalshi, ProphetX, possibly Novig, Polymarket US). Their legality is in active litigation, with a circuit split as of Sept 2026, and their liquidity on props is limited.

### Cited Findings
- **Pinnacle and Circa sharpness:**
  - Pinnacle offers NBA props at lower limits, so less sharp money shapes those lines. Some guides say high-volume US books can be sharper on certain props. Weak sources: [betherosports](https://betherosports.com/blog/how-to-use-pinnacle); [xclsvmedia, 2026](https://xclsvmedia.com/best-sharp-friendly-sportsbooks-2026-where-bet-when-limited/)
  - A Pikkit Dec 2024 sharpness ranking put Circa first on NBA main markets and near the top on props, but the sample was small. Circa posts only major prop markets it is confident in. Source: [Pikkit 2024](https://pikkit.com/blog/which-sportsbooks-are-sharp-best-apps-sports-betting-2024)
- **Prime Sports (a smaller US operator)** told Massachusetts regulators it does not limit players and uses sharp action to shape its lines. Source: [Covers, Sept 2024](https://www.covers.com/industry/massachusetts-limiting-sports-betting-debate-sharps-roundtable-september-2024)
- **Kalshi:**
  - Added NBA player props (points, rebounds, assists, threes) in mid-Nov 2025, for about 50 players.
  - Capped at $10,000 per trade per user.
  - Uses the integrity monitor IC360 and bars NBA players and staff from trading.
  - Source: [Front Office Sports, Nov 14, 2025](https://frontofficesports.com/article/kalshi-adds-nba-prop-markets-as-betting-crackdowns-surge/)
  - By May 2026, Covers was quoting Kalshi Wembanyama props, for example 25+ points at 62% implied, and said Kalshi was available in all 50 states. Source: [Covers, May 26, 2026](https://www.covers.com/nba/victor-wembanyama-props-at-kalshi-may-26-2026)
- **Kalshi litigation (circuit split):**
  - Third Circuit for Kalshi vs. New Jersey (Apr 2026).
  - Ninth Circuit 3-0 against Kalshi vs. Nevada (Aug 28, 2026). It held sports contracts are effectively sports bets and that federal commodities law likely does not preempt state law. The same court had denied Kalshi a stay in Mar 2026.
  - Sixth Circuit for Ohio and Tennessee (Sept 25, 2026). It held the contracts are not "swaps" and that state gambling law is not preempted.
  - New Jersey has asked the Supreme Court to resolve the split.
  - Sources: [Finance Magnates](https://financemagnates.com/fintech/federal-courts-now-disagree-on-kalshis-sports-contracts-new-jersey-wants-the-supreme-court-to-settle-the-fight); [Tennessee Lookout, Sept 28, 2026](https://tennesseelookout.com/2026/09/28/federal-court-rules-kalshi-is-subject-to-tennessees-gambling-laws-for-sports-contracts/); [CDC Gaming](https://cdcgaming.com/brief/nevada-ninth-circuit-denies-kalshi-stay-in-sports-contracts-case/)
- **Massachusetts** also sued Kalshi (referenced in [Front Office Sports, Nov 2025](https://frontofficesports.com/article/kalshi-adds-nba-prop-markets-as-betting-crackdowns-surge/)).
- **ProphetX:**
  - Received CFTC Designated Contract Market (DCM) and Derivatives Clearing Organization (DCO) approval around June 10–11, 2026.
  - Relaunched with player props.
  - Reported in 49 states (not Nevada).
  - Charges a 2% fee on net winnings for settled markets, per its help center as summarized by DeFiRate.
  - Sources: [Action Network review](https://www.actionnetwork.com/online-sports-betting/reviews/prophet-exchange); [DeFiRate](https://defirate.com/?p=13815)
- **Novig:**
  - Described as a CFTC DCM live in 47 states, with a nationwide launch listed for Aug 2026 ([Northeast Times](https://northeasttimes.com/?p=160920)).
  - Other pages describe it as a sweepstakes model. The sources conflict, and its status is unconfirmed.
  - Affiliate sites claim Novig has deeper player-prop liquidity than ProphetX and that exchanges have "no limits", with liquidity as the only ceiling. These claims are weak: [xclsvmedia](https://xclsvmedia.com/best-sharp-friendly-sportsbooks-2026-where-bet-when-limited/)
- **Polymarket** relaunched in the US in a limited beta in Nov 2025. **FanDuel and DraftKings** were preparing prediction-market products. Source: [Front Office Sports, Nov 2025](https://frontofficesports.com/article/kalshi-adds-nba-prop-markets-as-betting-crackdowns-surge/)

### Inferences
- **Reference prices:** For a two-person operation, the practical "true price" is a de-vigged consensus across several books, possibly weighted toward Circa or Pinnacle where they post the prop, plus exchange midpoints where volume exists. No single venue should be treated as truth for NBA props.
- **Exchanges as an outlet:** Exchanges remove limiting risk, but you take whatever price is resting or post your own and wait. Fees (for example, ProphetX's 2% on net winnings) take part of a 1–5% edge. Legal access depends on unresolved litigation: in Ninth- and Sixth-Circuit states (NV, OH, TN and others), state enforcement against Kalshi-style sports contracts now has appellate support.

### Gaps
- No verified current numbers on Pinnacle or Circa NBA prop limits.
- No liquidity or volume data for exchange NBA props.
- No current Kalshi fee schedule was retrieved.
- No BetCRIS or Bookmaker.eu prop-limit data. Both are offshore and illegal to use from US states with regulated markets; this is general knowledge, not sourced here.

## 3. DFS pick'em (PrizePicks, Underdog, Sleeper, DraftKings Pick6)

### Takeaway
Classic fixed payouts require about 54–60% per leg to break even:
- 2-pick at 3x needs 57.7% per leg.
- 5-pick at 20x needs 54.9%.
- 5- and 6-pick Flex need just over 54%.
- 3-pick Flex needs almost 60%.

Platforms increasingly adjust multipliers per pick or per entry. That reduces "stale line" edges, and pick'em is being pushed out of many states or turned into prediction-market products. I found no reliable reporting on pick'em operators limiting winners.

### Cited Findings
- **PrizePicks break-evens (Action Network, updated Dec 4, 2024):**
  - 2-leg Power at 3x: 57.74% per leg.
  - 5-leg Flex (10x / 2x / 0.4x) and 6-leg Flex (25x top tier): just over 54%.
  - 3-leg Flex: almost 60%.
  - Source: [Action Network](https://www.actionnetwork.com/education/prizepicks-flex-play-or-power-play-math)
- **Underdog Standard (2023–24 tables):** 2 picks 3x, 3 picks 6x, 4 picks 10x, 5 picks 20x. Sources: [Footballguys 2024](https://footballguys.com/article/2024-underdog-pick-em-week03); [Footballguys 2023](https://www.footballguys.com/article/2023-underdog-pick-em-week01)
  - A 5-pick at 20x breaks even at 54.9% per leg, about -122 American. Source: [Underdog Math, 2023](https://moods.beehiiv.com/p/ud-math-2023)
- **Underdog in 2026:** Multipliers "can change over time". Entry types are Standard (2–5 picks), Flex (3–8 picks, with 6+ picks double-flexed), and "Scorcher" boosted picks. Source: [Stokastic, June 2026](https://www.stokastic.com/articles/dfs-strategy/how-underdog-fantasy-multipliers-work)
- **DraftKings Pick6:** Multipliers vary by pick count and change during the day, but are locked when the entry is submitted. Reported availability is 29 states plus DC per Legal Sports Report, versus 35 plus DC per SportsLine. Sources: [SportsLine](https://www.sportsline.com/insiders/draftkings-pick6/); [Legal Sports Report](https://www.legalsportsreport.com/dfs-sites/draftkings-pick6/)
- **State availability (sources conflict):**
  - One list excludes PrizePicks paid contests from CO, CT, HI, ID, IA, LA, ME, MD, MI, MS, MT, NJ, NY, NV, OH, PA and WA ([Saturday Down South](https://www.saturdaydownsouth.com/dfs/legal-states/)).
  - Underdog Pick'em is reportedly in 15 states and its prediction markets in 38 (Aug 2026) ([lines.com](https://www.lines.com/guides/underdog-fantasy-states-legal-guide)).
  - Underdog's own help center lists states such as CT, HI, ID, IA and LA where only "Prediction Picks" are offered, not Pick'em ([Underdog help](https://help.underdogsports.com/en/articles/14127517-eligible-states-for-prediction-picks)).
- **Pick'em companies moving into prediction markets:** PrizePicks and Underdog are listed among prediction-market entrants in [Front Office Sports, Nov 2025](https://frontofficesports.com/article/kalshi-adds-nba-prop-markets-as-betting-crackdowns-surge/).
- **Line-setting:** Underdog is described as the "sharper" pick'em site, with lines tending to move toward Underdog's projection. Source: [Action Network explainer](https://www.actionnetwork.com/general/prizepicks-and-underdog-explainer-how-are-they-different-than-other-dfs-sites-sportsbooks)
- **Break-even caveat:** Fixed-multiplier break-evens apply only when all legs are equal. Platforms with lower lineup win rates imply looser per-leg break-evens on 3- and 5-pick entries. Source: [RotoGrinders](https://rotogrinders.com/grids/4033390)

### Inferences
- A model whose per-leg win probability is 54–58% is only marginally above pick'em break-even. Correlation and leg selection matter more than raw edge. The historical pick'em edge was taking sides where the fixed line was stale against sportsbook consensus at -125 or better. Dynamic multipliers and goblin/demon-style alternate lines erode that edge. The goblin/demon mechanics were not verified in this research.
- Pick'em lets you build entries from the strongest legs. However, the 1–5% edges described in the objective are measured against roughly -110 lines, which implies 52–55% win rates. That is mostly below pick'em break-even, so pick'em does not help unless the edge comes from stale pick'em lines specifically.

### Gaps
- Current (Oct 2026) official PrizePicks, Underdog, Sleeper and Pick6 payout tables were not retrieved. Sleeper was not researched.
- I found no credible reporting on pick'em operators limiting or banning winners. Max-entry caps exist but were not sourced.
- The California AG opinion on DFS pick'em (reportedly 2025) was not confirmed in this research.

## 4. Regulatory trends 2024–2026 affecting props

### Takeaway
The direction is toward more restriction on props:
- College player-prop bans in several states.
- League requests to cut role-player and pitch-level props after the 2025 scandals.
- Pending microbet bans.
- Per-bet taxes that raise the cost of small stakes.

The most aggressive proposal so far, Louisiana's full prop ban, died over lost revenue. Prediction markets are the main growth channel for props but face a circuit split.

### Cited Findings
- **College prop bans (2024):** Maryland, Ohio, Vermont and Louisiana banned collegiate prop bets; Massachusetts launched with a ban. Ohio's governor called for a ban on pro prop betting. Kentucky, NJ, NY and Utah were weighing restrictions. Source: [iGaming Business](https://igamingbusiness.com/gaming/us-gambling-legislation-sports-betting-update/)
- **BetMGM college-prop violation:** Massachusetts regulators said BetMGM took more than 15,000 college football player-prop wagers through same-game parlays, collecting more than $200,000 in violation of the state's ban. Source: [Casino.org](https://www.casino.org/news/massachusetts-regulators-say-betmgm-violated-player-prop/)
- **Louisiana SB 354 (2026):** Would have banned all prop and microbets from Aug 1. It died after a fiscal note estimated a $28.9M revenue loss. Sources: [Legal Sports Report](https://www.legalsportsreport.com/?p=259144); [SBC Americas, Mar 2026](https://sbcamericas.com/2026/03/03/louisiana-gambling-bills-2026/)
- **New Jersey S2160 / A3258:** A microbetting ban advanced from a Senate committee and moved to the Senate floor. It would be the first in the US. Final status is unknown. Sources: [CDC Gaming](https://cdcgaming.com/brief/new-jersey-senate-panel-advances-microbetting-ban/); [Fox Rothschild](https://www.foxrothschild.com/publications/micro-betting-is-a-full-ban-coming-to-new-jersey)
- **Illinois per-wager tax (effective July 1, 2025):**
  - $0.25 on each of an operator's first 20M bets per fiscal year, $0.50 after that.
  - FanDuel, DraftKings and Fanatics added per-bet surcharges for bettors. Other books raised minimum bets.
  - The Sports Betting Alliance linked the tax to 6.4M fewer bets in Oct 2025 versus Oct 2024.
  - A repeal bill was introduced.
  - Sources: [Covers, June 2025](https://www.covers.com/industry/illinois-sba-new-sports-betting-tax-per-bet-passed-june-2025); [Covers, July 2025](https://www.covers.com/industry/fanatics-illinois-sports-betting-surcharge-july-1-2025); [Covers, Jan 12, 2026](https://www.covers.com/industry/sports-betting-alliance-says-per-wager-tax-is-hurting-illinois-market-jan-12-2026)
- **NBA scandal follow-up:** League requests to cut low-minute player props (Oct 2025), and NBA/NFL/MLB prop reviews and restrictions (Nov 2025). Sources: see section 1.
- **Prediction markets:** Kalshi NBA props launched Nov 2025, followed by the circuit split in 2026. Sources: see section 2.

### Inferences
- **Illinois surcharge:** For small-stake prop bettors in Illinois, a flat per-bet surcharge on a $10 bet can wipe out a 1–5% edge entirely. The size of the surcharge was not retrieved, but even $0.25 is 2.5% of a $10 stake.
- **Thinning markets:** Bench and two-way player props, often the softest lines, are the markets leagues are pushing books to remove.

### Gaps
- Exact Illinois surcharge amounts per book, and the status of the repeal bill.
- Final status of NJ S2160 and any 2026 enacted pro-prop restrictions.
- Whether the NBA formally capped prop limits with its partner books (I found no operator announcement).

## 5. Tax and bookkeeping (brief)

### Takeaway
Starting with tax year 2026, only 90% of gambling losses are deductible, and only up to winnings (One Big Beautiful Bill Act, OBBBA). A break-even or slightly profitable bettor with high turnover can therefore owe tax on "phantom" income. Recreational bettors must also itemize to deduct losses at all.

### Cited Findings
- From tax year 2026 under OBBBA (IRC §165(d)):
  - Only 90% of gambling losses are deductible, capped at winnings, with no carryforward. Previously 100% was deductible.
  - Amateurs can deduct losses only if they itemize.
  - Professionals are also limited to 90% of losses plus expenses, so they can owe tax while breaking even.
  - Sources: [BPM](https://www.bpm.com/insights/new-gambling-loss-deduction-limits/); [CPA Practice Advisor, Sept 2025](https://www.cpapracticeadvisor.com/2025/09/02/a-raw-deal-for-gambling-loss-deductions-obbba/168188/); [Action Network](https://www.actionnetwork.com/news/new-obbba-gambling-losses-tax-now-in-effect)
- **Repeal efforts:** There were bipartisan efforts to repeal the 90% rule, but I found no confirmation that a fix passed. Source: [Action Network](https://www.actionnetwork.com/news/battle-over-gambling-tax-changes-in-one-big-beautiful-bill-heats-up)

### Inferences
- **Worked example:** A bettor who turns over $100k at a 3% edge has about $3k of profit. Under per-session netting it may be reported as, for example, $53k of winnings and $50k of losses. Only $45k of the losses would be deductible, giving $8k of taxable income on $3k of real profit.
- **What this means for planning:** Session-level netting and careful records (per-account statements, per-session logs) become essential. Whether gambling winnings count as "winnings" at the bet level or the session level matters a great deal. Treatment of prediction-market and exchange gains (possibly as Section 1256 or other contracts, versus gambling) is unsettled.

### Gaps
- How prediction-market and exchange profits are taxed (gambling, capital gains, or Section 1256) was not researched.
- Treatment of DFS pick'em winnings (1099-MISC) was not verified.
- State-specific taxes were not covered.
